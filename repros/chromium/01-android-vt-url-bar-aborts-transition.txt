Please describe the problem (5000 character limit)
Found while working on a lightbox component library.

document.startViewTransition() is skipped when the Android URL bar is still moving (show/hide animation, or innerHeight and visualViewport.height disagree):

InvalidStateError: Transition was aborted because of invalid state

Typical product sequence: a photo sits on a long page. Scroll so the URL bar is mid-animation, tap to open. Capture runs while innerHeight and visualViewport.height disagree. Close the overlay, scroll, open again.

That skip (ViewTransitionStyleTracker::SnapshotRootDidChangeSize, >1px) is specified for a real snapshot-containing-block resize (CSSWG 8045, crbug 1404957).

The bug is that Android's snapshot root is not invariant to URL-bar show/hide. Blink's View Transitions README says:

"The snapshot root is invariant with respect to whether viewport widgets are shown or not"

On Android (ViewportEnabled) it is FrameView size + browser-control outsets (TopShownRatio / BottomShownRatio). GetFixedToSnapshotViewportOutsets has a TODO that this needs synchronization with URL-bar animations:
https://github.com/chromium/chromium/commit/eeefabcd764a1ed3de9b0dcbd952de9fa0ca5474

Cross-document capture cancels the controls animation:
https://github.com/chromium/chromium/commit/34b55ca9b2e6045c65317cd82ac1337f7e63d4c4
Same-document startViewTransition() does not. Desktop Chromium and WebKit do not abort.

Expected: the transition completes (~UA duration) while the URL bar shows, hides, or is mid-animation. innerHeight vs visualViewport.height disagreeing should not skip it.

Actual:
- ready rejects with InvalidStateError, or
- ready fulfills, then finished resolves in ~8-50ms (skip after activation).

Do not use viewport-fit=cover on the test page; that overlays/hides the URL bar and the test passes.

A covering overlay that uses env(safe-area-inset-*) can also start a URL-bar twitch (filing separately). This issue is the abort from the bar moving, including from a user scroll with no env().

Workaround: wait until innerHeight and visualViewport.height agree and stay still before startViewTransition. Still races if the bar starts moving during capture.

Suggested fix: compute snapshot root from fully-shown or fully-hidden extents, not the in-flight shown-ratio, matching the README.

OS: Android 14 (Pixel 6 Pro; also Mac Android emulator).
Suggested components: Blink>ViewTransitions, Internals>Views.
