## Statutory withdrawal for consumers

If you are a consumer (an individual buying primarily for purposes outside your trade, business, craft, or profession), you may have a statutory right to withdraw from an online purchase under the law of your country.

For consumers in the EU/EEA, a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal generally applies to contracts concluded at a distance, counted from the day the contract is concluded. In Poland this right is set out in the Consumer Rights Act of 30 May 2014. We honor statutory withdrawal rights where they apply, and we do not ask you to waive them.

Where such a right applies:

- You do not have to give any reason.
- Withdrawing is free of charge.
- To meet the deadline, it is enough to send your withdrawal statement before the period expires.

Under Polish law, certain consumer rights, including withdrawal, also extend to an individual entrepreneur who enters into a contract directly related to their business activity when the contract is not of a professional character for them.

## Effect of withdrawal

If a statutory withdrawal right applies and you validly exercise it, the purchase contract is treated under applicable law as if it had not been concluded. In that case:

- We will refund all payments you made without undue delay, and no later than 14 days after receiving your withdrawal statement. The refund uses the same payment method you used, unless you expressly agree to a different method that costs you nothing; we process it through Whop, our payment processor, back to the original payment method.
- The paid proprietary license granted by that purchase is treated as never having taken effect, and you must stop relying on it.
- This does not affect the public availability of ramka or any rights you independently have under GPL-3.0-only. You may continue using ramka under the GPL, subject to its terms.

## Other refunds and reversals

The same license consequences apply if a purchase is refunded for any other reason, rescinded, or reversed, for example through a chargeback. The paid license granted by that purchase terminates, while rights independently available under GPL-3.0-only are unaffected. See the [Ramka License Agreement](https://ramka.dev/license/commercial).

Send complaints to [artur.bien+ramka@gmail.com](mailto:artur.bien+ramka@gmail.com) and we will respond as required by law.

We do not currently participate in a voluntary out-of-court consumer dispute resolution scheme. Depending on your country, out-of-court complaint options may still be available to you; in Poland, for example, through municipal consumer ombudsmen or the Trade Inspection.
