Ramka is a digital software license. A commercial license purchase grants the Licensee the rights described in the [Ramka Commercial License Agreement](https://ramka.dev/license/commercial). It is not a physical product and is not shipped.

## Non-refundable purchases

Once a commercial license has been granted, the purchase is generally non-refundable.

That includes a change of mind, unused licenses, and cases where the Licensee later chooses to use ramka under GPL-3.0-only instead.

## Exceptions required by law

Nothing in this policy limits any refund, withdrawal, or cancellation right you have under applicable law. If such a right applies, contact us and we will honour it.

## Duplicate charges, accidents, and wrong tier

Contact us if:

- you were charged more than once for the same order
- you purchased by accident
- you purchased the wrong license tier (for example Commercial instead of Agency)

We will review those requests and, where appropriate, refund, reverse a duplicate charge, or help you move to the correct tier.
