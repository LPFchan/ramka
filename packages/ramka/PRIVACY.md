# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Artur Bień**, operating under the business name Expensive Toys (“we”), collects and uses information in connection with ramka and the website at [ramka.dev](https://ramka.dev).

The `@ramka/react` library itself does not collect personal data from applications that use it. This policy covers the website, commercial licensing, and related support.

## Contact

The controller of personal data described in this policy is Artur Bień, operating under the business name Expensive Toys.

Questions, requests, and notices can be sent to [artur.bien+ramka@gmail.com](mailto:artur.bien+ramka@gmail.com).

## Information we collect

**Website analytics.** We use Vercel Analytics on ramka.dev to understand how the site is used, such as pages visited, referrers, and approximate device or browser information. Vercel describes this product as cookieless and designed not to collect personal information.

**Whop Pixel (only with your consent).** With your consent, we use the Whop Pixel to measure visits and conversions related to paid licenses. The pixel sets its own identifier in your browser using cookies or similar storage, may read certain advertising identifiers already present in your browser, and may associate your activity on ramka.dev with a later checkout on Whop. The pixel does not run unless you allow it through the consent notice shown on the site.

**Buyer, account, and contact data.** If you enquire about or purchase a paid license, or email us for support, we process the information you provide. That typically includes name, company or legal-entity name where applicable, billing email, license tier, and the content of your message. The [Ramka License Agreement](https://ramka.dev/license/commercial) records the Licensee identity against the order.

**Checkout and payment data.** Paid license purchases are processed by Whop. Whop collects and processes payment details and checkout information on our behalf. We receive the order information we need to grant and administer the license. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers.

## Purposes of processing

We process this information to:

- operate, secure, and improve ramka.dev
- measure site usage and, with your consent, the effectiveness of license offers
- take and fulfil paid license orders
- identify the Licensee and grant the correct license tier
- communicate about orders, licenses, and support
- handle withdrawal requests, refunds, and consumer complaints
- detect fraud, duplicate charges, and misuse
- meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases: your consent for the Whop Pixel (Article 6(1)(a)); performance of a contract for orders, licensing, withdrawal, and support (Article 6(1)(b)); compliance with legal obligations for tax, accounting, and consumer-law records (Article 6(1)(c)); and our legitimate interests in securing and improving the site and preventing fraud (Article 6(1)(f)).

## Cookies and tracking technologies

Vercel Analytics is designed to operate without cookies.

The Whop Pixel loads a script from Whop and uses cookies, local storage, or similar identifiers to recognize browsers, attribute visits, and measure conversions. It runs only if you allow it through the consent notice shown on the site. Your choice is stored in your browser so we do not ask again; you can change it at any time using the tracking preferences link in the site footer, and a withdrawn consent stops the pixel from loading on subsequent page views. Whop’s use of those technologies is described in [Whop’s Privacy Policy](https://whop.com/privacy/).

The website may also store a theme preference in your browser (for example in local storage) so the interface can stay in light or dark mode. That preference remains on your device.

You can additionally block or delete cookies and similar storage in your browser settings. Declining or blocking the Whop Pixel may prevent us from attributing a purchase; it does not prevent you from using the documentation or purchasing a license.
