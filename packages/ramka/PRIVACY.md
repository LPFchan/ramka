# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Artur Bień**, operating under the business name Expensive Toys (“we”), collects and uses information in connection with ramka and the website at [ramka.dev](https://ramka.dev).

The `@ramka/react` library itself does not collect personal data from applications that use it. This policy covers the website, commercial licensing, and related support.

## Contact

The controller of personal data described in this policy is Artur Bień, operating under the business name Expensive Toys.

Questions, requests, and notices can be sent to [artur.bien+ramka@gmail.com](mailto:artur.bien+ramka@gmail.com).

## Information we collect

**Website analytics.** We use Vercel Analytics on ramka.dev to understand how the site is used, such as pages visited, referrers, and approximate device or browser information. Vercel describes this product as cookieless and designed not to collect personal information.

**Whop Pixel.** We use the Whop Pixel to measure visits and conversions related to commercial licenses. The pixel may collect information about your browser, device, and activity on ramka.dev, and may associate that activity with a later checkout on Whop.

**Buyer, account, and contact data.** If you enquire about or purchase a commercial license, or email us for support, we process the information you provide. That typically includes name, company or legal-entity name, billing email, license tier, and the content of your message. The [Ramka Commercial License Agreement](https://ramka.dev/license/commercial) records the Licensee identity against the order.

**Checkout and payment data.** Commercial license purchases are processed by Whop. Whop collects and processes payment details and checkout information on our behalf. We receive the order information we need to grant and administer the license. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers.

## Purposes of processing

We process this information to:

- operate, secure, and improve ramka.dev
- measure site usage and the effectiveness of license offers
- take and fulfil commercial license orders
- identify the Licensee and grant the correct license tier
- communicate about orders, licenses, and support
- detect fraud, duplicate charges, and misuse
- meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

## Cookies and tracking technologies

Vercel Analytics is designed to operate without cookies.

The Whop Pixel loads a script from Whop and may use cookies, local storage, or similar identifiers to recognize browsers, attribute visits, and measure conversions. Whop’s use of those technologies is described in [Whop’s Privacy Policy](https://whop.com/privacy/).

The website may also store a theme preference in your browser (for example in local storage) so the interface can stay in light or dark mode. That preference remains on your device.

You can block or delete cookies and similar storage in your browser settings. Blocking the Whop Pixel may prevent us from attributing a purchase; it does not prevent you from using the documentation or purchasing a license.

## Third-party processors

We use the following processors:

- **Vercel** — hosts ramka.dev and provides Vercel Analytics. See [Vercel’s Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Whop** — provides checkout, payment processing, and the Whop Pixel. See [Whop’s Privacy Policy](https://whop.com/privacy/).

Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. They may process data outside your country of residence.

## Retention

We keep license and purchase records for as long as needed to provide the perpetual commercial license and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Support and enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up.

Analytics and pixel data are retained according to the relevant processor’s practices.
